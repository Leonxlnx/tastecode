# Code of conduct

TasteCode is built in public by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Participation requires respectful, constructive, and professional behavior.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people
- give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith
- respect privacy, boundaries, and different perspectives
- keep project spaces free from harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted attention
- avoid publishing another person's private information

## Scope

These expectations apply in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project chat, and
public spaces where someone is representing TasteCode.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[hello@tasteskill.dev](mailto:hello@tasteskill.dev). Do not post sensitive reports publicly.
Include only the context needed to understand the incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe and productive project environment.
Reports should be handled as privately as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest
avoided.
