## Product and interaction references

These projects informed product research or specific interaction decisions. Personal
Harness is not a fork of them.

- [OpenAI Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex) — the `Thread -> Turn -> Item` domain
  model and the app-server integration surface. Types under
  `packages/adapter-codex/src/generated/` are generated by Codex app-server.
- [T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) — control-plane architecture, scroll-mode
  vocabulary, and composer transition research.
- [OpenCode](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode) — an independent reference for a local
  server with thin clients and multiple agent backends.
- The Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Conductor, and Nimbalyst products were used as competitive
  references for workflows and visual direction.

Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Mentioning a product here
does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

TanStack Virtual, xterm.js, Lucide, Geist, node-pty, Zod, and ws. Visual details also use
