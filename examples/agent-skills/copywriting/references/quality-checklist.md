# Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering, committing, or publishing copy.

## Scope and accuracy

- Did I preserve the requested meaning and facts?
- Did I keep names, dates, titles, specifications, links, and qualifiers accurate?
- Did I avoid adding claims, metrics, services, locations, or credentials?
- Did I leave testimonials, legal text, pricing, safety instructions, accessibility requirements, and approved or locked copy unchanged?
- When editing code or CMS content, did I preserve field names, IDs, arrays, routes, and behavior?
- If text is split across fields, does the rendered sentence read correctly?

## Language, audience, and inclusion

- Did I use the requested language or preserve the source language?
- Did I keep proper nouns, technical terms, commands, and interface labels accurate?
- Did I avoid assumptions about identity, ability, culture, family, location, device, or expertise?
- Are instructions understandable without relying only on color, position, sound, motion, hover, or drag?
- Will the wording translate cleanly when localization is likely?

## Voice

- Does the copy sound like a person who knows the subject?
- Is it direct without sounding abrupt?
- Is it professional without corporate filler?
- Does the personality come from real details rather than slogans?
- Are contractions used naturally where appropriate?

## Sentence openings and rhythm

- Do several nearby sentences begin with `I`, `We`, `This`, `Our`, or `The`?
- Can one sentence begin with context, time, the project, the role, or the result?
- Did I keep some first-person language where ownership matters?
- Did I avoid awkward clauses or passive voice created only for variety?
- Are sentence lengths reasonably varied?

## Clarity and concision

- Does each section lead with its main point?
- Can any sentence lose words without losing meaning?
- Are subjects and pronouns clear?
- Did I remove duplicate ideas?
- Are paragraphs focused?
- Are headings literal and useful?
- Did I remove eyebrow, overline, kicker, or preheader labels that only repeat a heading?
- Do remaining labels add useful metadata or a true process step, or follow an explicit request or locked design system?
- Are CTAs specific?
- Are unordered cards and lists free of decorative numbering?
- If numbering is used, does order, rank, count, or reference make it useful?
- Did I avoid leading zeroes unless they are part of a real identifier, standard, locked design, or explicit request?

## AI-pattern check

- No em dashes
- No generic modern-world opening
- No `This is X, not Y` structure
- No `not just X, but Y` structure
- No `more than just` filler
- No `where X meets Y` slogan
- No empty use of `elevate`, `unlock`, `empower`, `transform`, or `reimagine`
- No unsupported superlatives
- No forced groups of three
- No redundant eyebrow headings or label-plus-heading pairs
- No numbered cards or sections used only for visual structure
- No decorative leading-zero labels such as `01`, `02`, and `03`
- No generic conclusion that repeats the introduction
- No cute interface copy that hides the task or recovery step

## SEO, when applicable

- Does the title describe the page?
- Is the main topic present naturally in the H1 or opening copy?
- Does the page answer the likely search question early?
- Are headings descriptive?
- Is the meta description unique, accurate, and readable?
- Is internal-link text descriptive?
- Are locations and claims supported?
- Did I avoid keyword stuffing and SEO filler?

## Final delivery

- Is the format ready to paste, implement, or review?
- If this is an incremental request, did I include only the new changes?
- If I edited a repository, did I run the relevant checks?
- Did I report exactly what changed and what remained untouched?
